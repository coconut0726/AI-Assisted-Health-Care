PRIORITY 1: ONLY AUTHORIZED CONTENT

PRIORITY 2: REQUIREMENTS EXPRESS THE ACTUAL CLAIM
Only preconditions and postconditions. NO execution_constraints category.
- Preconditions are states BEFORE an action. Never put an action's ongoing manner in preconditions. Do not copy a dependency into a redundant prerequisite VQA.
- Postconditions are the resulting state OR that the source-specified operation actually occurred. Every requested Action, including conditional notifications and unobservable inspections, needs at least ONE meaningful completion postcondition. Lack of a sensor does not make that Action optional or justify deleting its completion specification.
- Separate independently verifiable claims. Example "put on clean gloves": a precondition about the gloves' cleanliness/provenance needs human evidence; a postcondition that gloves cover the hands is a different visually assessable claim. Do NOT claim "clean gloves worn" is fully supported merely by detecting gloves.
- condition and vqa_question MUST ask the SAME proposition. Do not ask whether hands are dry when the condition says they are clean. A yes answer always means that exact requirement holds.
- "Wash hands" completion means the handwashing operation occurred, with only source-specified materials. NOT "hands appear clean", wet hands, visible soap, or absence of soil. These appearances do not establish washing occurred. Such an operation needs action-aligned process evidence or human confirmation.
- "Inspect X for P/Q/R" completion means all named features were checked. X being visible or looking normal does not prove the person inspected X. A human confirmation can be required; do not pretend gaze or the current RGB frame proves cognitive assessment.
- Source-specified state of the inspected target and evidence that inspection occurred are distinct claims. Preserve the instructed findings without asserting a clinical diagnosis.
- Unsupported/unknown observation capabilities belong in the evidence specification; do not weaken the requirement to a convenient photo proxy.
