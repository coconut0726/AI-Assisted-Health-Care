Only preconditions, postconditions and error_criteria. NO execution_constraints. Each requested Action MUST have a completion postcondition, even when it requires unavailable evidence or conditional human confirmation.

Source fidelity:
- Use only original_instruction and actually supplied expert notes. Empty nurse_prior means expert_prior is FORBIDDEN. A graph is a draft, not authorization to invent clinical requirements.
- explicit_instruction requires a single exact contiguous quote as source_reference. graph_dependency requires concrete IDs and the relation. inferred requires requires_expert_review=true. Prefer not generating inferred conditions.
- No added objects, washing agents, glove-removal techniques, clinical diagnoses/risks, force limits, temperature ranges, durations or tolerances. skip_impact explains dependency/task-outcome effects only; do not discuss infection, asepsis, blood flow etc unless explicitly supplied.

Make every condition, question and evidence specification express the SAME claim. A yes answer means that whole claim holds. Separate state claims that need different evidence. Missing evidence never changes the source meaning.

Mandatory examples:
1. "Wash hands": completion = the handwashing operation occurred. NOT hands appear clean/wet, absence of dirt or supplies nearby. Use human confirmation or adequate action video/history; the current single-frame app cannot establish occurrence.
2. "Put on clean gloves": cleanliness of gloves is a source-supported PREcondition requiring human/provenance evidence; gloves worn on hands is the separate visible POSTcondition. Do NOT combine these into a supposedly visible clean-gloves claim. Do NOT add a precondition repeating the graph's earlier handwashing dependency.
3. "Inspect X for P,Q,R": completion = the user checked all three named features. Human confirmation may be necessary. X being visible, camera gaze direction or apparent absence of P/Q/R does not prove the inspection occurred. Any separate target-state finding is distinct and does not equal a clinical diagnosis.
4. A conditional notification still needs a completion postcondition specifying the source-requested notification was made when its trigger held. Do NOT return no requirements or make it optional because the current camera cannot verify it.
5. A manner term (e.g., gently), material qualifier, tool-use rule, quantitative qualifier (e.g., thin), tightness qualifier (e.g., loosely) or prohibition must remain in a requirement or a specific error criterion. Do not drop it merely into review_notes. Undefined qualitative interpretation requires expert review; do not invent a measurement or test maneuver.

Categories:
- Preconditions are states before the Action, not its ongoing behavior. Do not duplicate graph edges as VQAs.
- Postconditions are actual resulting states or actual completion of the specified operation. A plausible end state does not establish action history. Every Action has at least one.
- Local error criteria diagnose only source-supported wrong_object,spatial_error,technique_error,measurement_error,timing_parameter_error. No generic imaginary mistakes. Not doing the Action is not Wrong Object; that belongs to Missing Step. Existing requirement IDs may be referenced; [] is allowed for a standalone explicit execution rule. No duplicate requirement merely to manufacture an ID.
- Every criterion states a specific violation, sufficient positive compliance, and a matching positive compliance question. No detected violation is not proof of compliance. Inherit the Action's applicability. False is not applicable, unknown unresolved.
- A cross-action prohibition belongs once in globals, never copied locally. An Action-specific rule stays local. Do not combine different rules into one question.

Evidence:
Use schema fields and enums. Describe evidence sufficient for the WHOLE claim, its time window and limitations. current_support: supported only if all needed evidence is supplied; partial if relevant but inconclusive evidence is supplied; unsupported if required process/history/sensor data are missing; human_required if human confirmation is needed (the current app has no such channel).
- Single after_step RGB is not aligned before_action evidence, video, hand joints, contact measurements, event history or a physical sensor. Before checks and temporal operations are not fully supported by this app.
- Surface appearance does not establish cleanliness, material non-stick property, temperature, tension, smell, fever or successful communication. Explicit human/provenance evidence may be needed.
- Do not infer contact/containment/coverage from 2D overlap/boxes, grasping from co-motion, or use from a nearby tool. Occluded target boundaries/interfaces can make pad coverage, tape position and seal engagement unknown.
- Continuous prohibitions require sufficient observation of the relevant whole interval; a compliant final photo or absence of visible change cannot establish no earlier violation. Sparse image timestamps do not measure action duration.
- For quantitative/qualitative uncertainty retain the requirement, mark review, and explain the missing definition. Never output satisfied/failed/runtime status when no execution observations were supplied.
