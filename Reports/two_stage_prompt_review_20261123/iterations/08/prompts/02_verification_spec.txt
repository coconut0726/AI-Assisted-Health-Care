SOURCE RULES
- Original instruction is primary; graph is a draft. Use only supplied expert notes, never generic medical practice. Empty nurse_prior gives NO expert authority.
- explicit_instruction: source_reference must be ONE EXACT CONTIGUOUS QUOTE from original_instruction. graph_dependency: cite exact IDs AND the actual relation. inferred: only logically necessary inference, ALWAYS requires_expert_review=true. Prefer [] over unnecessary inferred requirements.
- No invented objects, materials, action substeps, diagnoses, clinical safety rules, thresholds, doses, times, pressure limits or manual test maneuvers. Clean does not automatically mean sterile or unused; thin does not imply an extra uniformity rule.

PRE / POST
- A precondition is a source-supported state before the Action. Do NOT repeat a graph dependency as another requirement. No invented camera-visibility preconditions.
- Every requested Action MUST have a meaningful completion postcondition, including inspections and conditional notifications. Do not delete an unobservable requirement to fit the current camera.
- A final physical state and the occurrence of an operation are different. For example, hands appearing clean/wet does not prove handwashing; a tool nearby does not prove use. When occurrence matters, specify that the actual source-defined operation occurred and require process or human evidence.
- Preserve EVERY source qualifier in actual requirements/criteria, not just review_notes. Split properties needing different evidence. For clean gloves, a human/provenance precondition establishes clean gloves; a separate RGB postcondition can establish gloves worn. Non-stick material identity similarly needs provenance/label/human evidence, distinct from visible pad placement.
- Source-specified clean water/towel is not visually established by appearance. Cool temperature, loose tension and thin layer have no supplied quantitative definition: preserve the words, set requires_expert_review=true for that interpretation, require adequate human/sensor/process evidence without adding a procedural measuring Action.
- Inspecting a target for listed features requires checking ALL those features. Image appearance or camera pointing does not prove the person performed the cognitive check. Author inspection-completion evidence separately from target-state findings; do not assert clinical clearance from RGB alone.
- A conditional call/notification remains required when its condition holds, without waiting for unrelated steps. Use human/communication evidence. No invented contact tool, nurse acknowledgment requirement, response deadline or later-only restriction.

ERROR RULES
- Taxonomy ONLY: order_error, missing_step, wrong_object, spatial_error, technique_error, measurement_error, timing_parameter_error.
- Local errors may use latter five only. Globals handle order/missing and cross-action rules.
- By default error_criteria is []. Add only a concrete source-based manner/prohibition or a useful specific diagnostic of an existing requirement. Do not invent extra mistakes merely to fill taxonomy; do not duplicate every postcondition in negative form.
- Wrong Object requires actual use of an identified wrong object, not a missing visible object. Missing performance belongs to missing_step. A missing negative observation never proves positive compliance.
- Keep violation_condition and compliance_condition logically opposed about the SAME behavior. E.g. prohibited rubbing is violated by rubbing the burn, not by any physical contact. A gentle manner is not an invented speed/force threshold.
- One cross-action rule is stored once globally, not also locally. Distinct prohibitions stay distinct. An action-specific tape-placement rule stays local; keeping tape OFF burned skin does not require tape ONLY on gauze.
- Inherit Action applicability for local criteria. False applicability => not applicable, unknown => unresolved. Do not treat conditional instructions as optional.

Use the schema fields: evidence_types,relevant_entities,observable_cues,temporal_scope,verification_method,observability,current_support,limitations.
Describe evidence SUFFICIENT for the whole claim, not convenient superficial cues. Observable cues for human_confirmation should specify the confirmation/record needed, not pretend that a person's visual guess proves material history.
Against supplied current_system_capabilities:

- partial: some relevant evidence is supplied but the complete claim is unresolved.
- unsupported: necessary process/history/sensor evidence is absent.
- human_required: human confirmation is necessary; the app does NOT currently collect it.
Only after_step RGB snapshots are currently supplied, no aligned before views, action video/history, gaze, hand-contact, physical temperature/force measurements. Do not claim these are available.
No cleanliness/non-stick/temperature/tension/odor/fever/communication proof from appearance. No contact/containment/coverage proof from projection overlap or bounding boxes. Target/interface occlusion can make pad coverage, tape position and seal engagement inconclusive.
A temporal claim requires adequate observation/records over its relevant interval; a final intact-looking scene cannot prove no earlier violation. Sparse image timestamps are not operation duration. Use human_required or unsupported for missing essential evidence; unknown will remain unknown at runtime.
